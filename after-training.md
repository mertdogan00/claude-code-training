# After the training: your first week

Day 1 (tonight, 20 minutes): install Claude Code on YOUR machine (`docs/setup/`), clone this
repo, redo the Stage 3 exercise. Feel the CLAUDE.md difference once more, on your own screen.

Day 2-3: pick the most boring 15-minute task you did this week. Describe it to Claude Code in
Turkish. Iterate until the output is genuinely usable. Save the working prompt into a file.

Day 4-5: turn that prompt into a skill (Stage 4a shows how). Now it is a `/command`.

Weekend: Scenario 3: one sitting, one small product. Show it to someone.

The trap to avoid: trying to automate your WHOLE job in week one. The people who get real
value add one small piece at a time and write their rules into `CLAUDE.md` as they learn
them. Sessions forget; files do not.

Stuck? The answer is usually better CONTEXT, not a better model: say what you want, what you
have, and what "good" looks like.
