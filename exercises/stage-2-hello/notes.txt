I run a small cafe in Ankara, near Tunali, twelve tables and three people on staff.
Every Monday the coffee prices move again and I retype the whole menu in three places: the printed card, the board outside and the photo we put on Instagram.
It eats half my morning and I still miss a line somewhere.
Then there are the supplier emails: milk, beans, the bakery, the cleaning company.
The same four questions every week, and I answer them at midnight when I am too tired to be polite.
On Sundays I sit with the till printouts and try to work out which days were good and what actually sold.
I know the answer is in there somewhere, I just never have the patience to add it all up.
People also keep telling me we should post more on social media.
I have the photos on my phone, but I never know what to write under them, so most of them never go up.
If something could take the menu updates, the supplier replies, a short weekly sales summary and a few post captions off my hands, I would get my Sunday back.
